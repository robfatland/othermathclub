# Writing Style

Applies to all documentation, READMEs, and book text written for this repo.

## Be concise

Favor the shorter phrasing. When writing or revising documentation, actively look for words to
cut. Prefer plain statements over throat-clearing and emphasis padding.

- Cut filler and self-promoting labels ("the headline skill", "the key thing to note", "it is
  important that"). State the thing; let it carry its own weight.
- One idea per sentence. Short sentences over long ones.
- Prefer a concrete example over a sentence describing what the example would show.
- In tables, keep cells terse — fragments, not sentences.
- Don't restate the heading in the first line under it.

This reflects a standing instruction from Rob and the project's own principle: students will
not read lengthy descriptions. Keep written material minimal and punchy.
